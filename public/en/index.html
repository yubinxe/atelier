The problem

More AI tools. The same way of working.

Most inefficiency is not a lack of technology. It comes from work that is broken into pieces and stitched together by hand.

Repetitive data gathering

The same figures are requested from several places every week and pasted together by hand.

Manual document handling

Reading, classifying and re-keying incoming documents is still someone's job.

Broken information flow

Information moves between teams and systems only by email and chat, and no one is sure which version is current.

AI output no one can verify

There is no record of whether an AI result was right or who checked it.

Automation no one maintains

When an automation fails no one notices, and without its author no one can fix it.
