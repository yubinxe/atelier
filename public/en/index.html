The problem

More AI tools, the same way of working

In most organisations AI stays a personal productivity tool. If the structure of the work does not change, neither do the results.

  1. 01

    The tools sit outside the work

    AI used individually is not connected to the organisation's procedures, data or approvals. People still carry the results across by hand.

  2. 02

    There is no way to check the result

    Nothing records whether an AI output was right or who verified it — so it is kept away from the work that matters most.

  3. 03

    Automation has no owner

    Once built, an automation can fail without anyone noticing, and changing it means finding whoever made it.

Our approach

We design the work before choosing the tools.

Before deciding which model to use, we look at where the work starts, whom it passes through and where it ends. On that map we divide roles between AI and people, build working software, and keep it verifiable in operation.
