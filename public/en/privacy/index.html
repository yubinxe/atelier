Privacy policy

1. Data we collect and why

Atelier AX (“we”) collects the following through the contact form.

ItemsName, work email and message (required) · organisation, service of interest and project stage (optional)
PurposeReviewing and replying to enquiries and holding consultations
RetentionOne year after the enquiry is closed, then deleted without delay, unless a law requires longer retention.

We do not collect data for accounts, payments or advertising.

2. Information collected automatically

This site uses no analytics or advertising cookies. Studio Demo records and display settings are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent to us. Our hosting provider may briefly process access logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and operation.

3. Processors and international transfer

Vercel Inc. (USA)Website hosting and server-side handling of the contact form; enquiry data may pass through servers in the United States.
Supabase Inc. (USA)Storage of enquiries, kept in the Seoul region (ap-northeast-2).

We do not provide personal data to third parties for any other purpose.

4. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or to stop its processing, at any time. Send your request through the contact form and we will act on it promptly after verifying your identity.

5. Security

Enquiries are stored where public access is blocked, with access limited to the minimum. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS.

6. Data protection officer

The owner of Atelier AX · Contact: the contact form on this site

7. Effective date

This policy applies from 6 November 2026. Changes will be posted on this page.
