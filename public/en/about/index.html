About

Complex work, made clear and controllable.

Atelier AX is an AI transformation studio founded in Seoul in 2026 as a sole proprietorship. As the name suggests, we look closely at each client's work and build a system that fits it precisely — working with the focus and execution of a small studio rather than imitating a large firm.

Mission

Turn complex work into clearer, controllable systems.

Work usually becomes complex not because there is too much of it, but because its flow cannot be seen. We make the flow visible, divide it between people and AI, and make the results checkable.

Vision

An environment where AI contributes lastingly to productivity and decisions.

Not AI that ends with a single demo, but AI that is recorded, verified and improved inside everyday work.

Principles

Four principles behind how we work

  1. 01

    Technology serves operations

    We judge technology by how much it actually changes everyday work, not by how new it is.

  2. 02

    Intelligence is useful once verified

    We separate an AI saying it is done from something that actually works. A check that has not run stays unverified.

  3. 03

    Every automation needs an owner

    Each automation needs a point where it stops, a person who checks it and a way to roll it back.

  4. 04

    Design is the structure of execution

    Screens and sentences, data structures and approval steps are treated as one design. Well-designed work needs less explaining.
