AX Strategy & Process Intelligence

Decide where AI should go first — with evidence, not hype.

The problem
There is pressure to adopt AI, but no basis for deciding which work to change first or what will actually pay off.
What we do
  • Interviews and a survey of document and data flows
  • Breaking work into repeatable, judgement and approval steps
  • Assessing automation potential and risk
  • A prioritised, phased transformation roadmap
Deliverables
  • Current and target process maps
  • Automation candidates with an assessment matrix
  • Phased roadmap
  • A definition of the first project
Typical applications
  • Mapping a review-heavy team to find its bottlenecks
  • Scoping automation for recurring reporting work

AI Workflow Automation

Turn repetitive collecting, formatting and hand-offs into automation that leaves a record.

The problem
The same gathering, tidying and forwarding is repeated by hand every week, and data moves between systems by copy and paste.
What we do
  • Automating repetitive work and connecting systems
  • Implementation on workflow tools such as n8n
  • Dividing roles between AI agents and placing human approval steps
  • Failure alerts and retry rules
Deliverables
  • A workflow running in production
  • Run history and failure alerts
  • An operating and change manual
Typical applications
  • Collecting branch sales data and sending a weekly report
  • Gathering notices and documents, then summarising and classifying them
How to start
We walk through one task you currently do by hand and scope the automation from there. Get in touch →

04

AI Governance & Operational Reliability

Decide who checks what AI has done, and where it must stop.

The problem
No one has defined who verified an AI-generated result, or where the process stops and rolls back when something goes wrong.
What we do
  • Execution tracing and record structure
  • Designing the points where a person must approve
  • Verification procedures and quality criteria
  • Error handling and rollback; separating permissions and secrets
Deliverables
  • Approval and verification policy
  • Execution record structure
  • Operating checklist
  • A monitoring view
Typical applications
  • A gate that requires human approval before anything is published
  • A results table that separates AI judgement from executed checks
